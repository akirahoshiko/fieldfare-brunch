Your privacy

Last updated 17 November 2026. This page explains what we do with the details you give us when you order or join our mailing list. In short: we only use them to make your order and keep you posted, and we never sell them.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, phone number, your order and any note for the kitchen. Your email too, if you choose to give it.
  • When you join the mailing list: your email address.
  • On this device: your order in progress and the details you typed, so they're still there if you leave the page. These stay in your browser and you can clear them at any time.

Why we use it

  • To prepare your order and call out your name when it's ready.
  • To phone you if there's a problem with your order.
  • To email your confirmation and tell you when your order is ready, if you gave an email.
  • To send occasional news about specials and opening times, only if you joined the mailing list.

Who else handles it

The website runs on Cloudflare. Order emails are sent with Google Gmail, and our order log is kept in Google Sheets. They handle your details only to provide those services to us. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

Orders are kept for 12 months for our records, then deleted automatically. You stay on the mailing list until you ask to leave.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, or to leave the mailing list, at any time. Email peaceomachunyiejiga@gmail.com and we'll do it within a month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
