    Good to know

    Questions we get asked

    Something else on your mind? Ask us at the counter, we're happy to help.

    Do you deliver?

    No, we're pickup only. Order online, choose a time, and collect from the counter on Church Street.

    How do I pay?

    At the counter when you collect, by card or cash. Nothing is charged online.

    How will I know my order is ready?

    After you order you get your own order page that updates by itself: received, being prepared, ready. If you give us your email, we'll also email you when it's ready.

    I have an allergy. Can I still order?

    Every dish lists its allergens, and you can add a note for the kitchen. Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten and dairy, so we can't promise anything is completely free of them. If your allergy is severe, please talk to us before ordering.

    Can I order for later, or for tomorrow?

    Yes. Pick any 15-minute time that's still open. When we're closed, you can order ahead for our next opening day.

    What if I need to change my order?

    Tell us when you arrive, or place a new order and mention it at the counter. If the kitchen hasn't started yet, we'll happily change it.
