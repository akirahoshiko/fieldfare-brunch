About us

A small kitchen with a long table

Fieldfare is a twelve-seat brunch spot on Church Street. Most weekends there's a queue out the door by ten, so we made it easy to order ahead and walk straight in.

Our bread comes from a bakery down the road, the coffee is roasted in London, and the menu changes with the seasons.
